Real-time voice → img2img pipeline. Whisper transcribes your mic, feeds live verbal prompts to FLUX2-klein via ComfyUI, webcam realtime image feed transforms at ~3fps. No buttons. Just speaking the images into existence.  Code/details avail on Github.
